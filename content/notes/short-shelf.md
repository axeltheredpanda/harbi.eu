A portfolio with twelve half-described projects tells a recruiter nothing. A shelf with two honest ones tells them how you think.

Axel Project and Astraia sit there because I shaped them. They're not polished case studies yet — and I'm fine saying that out loud. The point of this site isn't to invent outcomes. It's to leave a clear trail of taste, craft, and follow-through.
