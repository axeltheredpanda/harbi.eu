---
title: Building in public, quietly
date: 2026-07-10
excerpt: A short note on shipping a personal site without turning it into a product launch.
---

Most personal sites die of ambition. Too many sections, too many promises, one weekend of energy.

I wanted harbi.eu to stay small on purpose: a readable landing for recruiters, a private corner for myself, and enough personality that it doesn't feel like a template wearing a tie.

The hard part isn't the stack. It's deciding what *not* to build yet — and leaving room for the next idea without pretending the current page is finished forever.
